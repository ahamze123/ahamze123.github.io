# Dragon Monsoon

A sword and sorcery adventure for 1 to 3 players, made from Street Monsoon's engine in the spirit of Golden Axe. Korr, Sera and Bram fight the Dragon Lord's army across five lands to bring the Sunstone back to the kingdom of Lumen. **Play:** https://ahamze123.github.io/dragon-monsoon/

## The heroes
- **KORR** (balanced): a barbarian with a giant sword. Three-hit combo, a spinning special, and the magic **STORM CALL**: lightning falls on every enemy on the screen.
- **SERA** (speed): the quickest blade, a rising slash to finish her combo, and the magic **FROST STORM**: ice bursts up under every enemy and freezes them.
- **BRAM** (power): a dwarf smith with a mighty hammer. His special shakes the ground, and his magic **DRAGON FIRE** sends a dragon of flame across the screen.

The fighters were drawn in ChatGPT from the front, the side and the back, turned into 3D models with Meshy (multi-image to 3D) and given their fighting moves, then drawn as cel-shaded pictures for every frame. Every other picture was drawn in ChatGPT too: the five lands (far views and nearby rows), the floors, the lanterns, carts, wells, mine carts, banners and statues, the barrels, crates and pots, and the food, gold and magic jars you pick up.

## The five lands
1. **Meadowbrook**, a sleepy village (boss: GRUMBO the ogre)
2. **The Whisperwood**, a forest of glowing mushrooms (boss: the BONE GIANT)
3. **The Sky Whale**, a ship on the back of a flying whale (boss: CAPTAIN VEX)
4. **The Ember Mines** (boss: MAGMA GRUMBO)
5. **The Storm Citadel** (boss: DRAXX, the Dragon Lord)

## How to play
- **Attack** again and again for a combo. Run (tap a direction twice) and attack for a shoulder charge. Jump and attack for a flying slash.
- Walk into an enemy to **grab** him: attack to bash him, attack after two bashes (or attack while pushing back) to throw him.
- **Special**: a spinning attack all around you. It costs a little health, but never your last bit.
- **Magic**: uses every magic jar you carry at once; the more jars, the bigger the spell. Kick the little **thieves** (blue hats carry magic jars, green hats carry food) to shake jars and food out of their sacks.
- Barrels, crates and pots hide food, gold, gems and magic jars.

## Controls
- **Keyboard (1 player):** arrows or WASD to move, **J** attack, **K** or **Space** jump, **L** special, **E** magic. **P** or **Esc** pauses.
- **2 players on one keyboard:** P1 uses WASD + **F** attack, **G** jump, **H** special, **T** magic. P2 uses the arrows + **J** attack, **K** jump, **L** special, **I** magic.
- **Gamepad:** stick or d-pad moves, **X** attack, **A** jump, **B** special, **Y** magic, **Start** pauses.
- **Touch:** left thumb moves; on the right are ATTACK, JUMP, SPECIAL and MAGIC.

## Online co-op (up to 3 players)
1. Everyone opens https://ahamze123.github.io/dragon-monsoon/ and picks **ONLINE CO-OP** on the title screen.
2. One player picks **HOST A GAME** and gets a 4-letter room code.
3. Friends pick **JOIN A GAME** and type that code (or tap ▲ ▼ on each letter). Each player picks a different hero.
4. The host picks the level and presses **START!**.

The host's computer runs the game; friends' buttons go to it and every screen shows the same adventure, with the same sounds and effects. If a friend leaves, their hero leaves and the game goes on. The menu (P or Esc) does not stop the game for the others. Online play needs an internet connection and works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

## Full screen
- The game switches to full screen when you start playing (turn it off with **FULL SCREEN** on the title screen or in the pause menu).
- On an iPad or iPhone, for the best full screen: open the page in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. The game then opens like an app, full screen and sideways.
