# Iron Monsoon

An original run-and-gun adventure in bright painted worlds. Two heroes, Volt and Ember, fight the robot army of the Overseer. **Play:** https://ahamze123.github.io/iron-monsoon/

## Story and missions
Pick **STORY MODE** on the title screen to play the whole adventure, with Volt, Ember, Colonel Hawk, Dr. Nova and the little helper drone Bolt talking between missions. You can also pick any mission you have reached and play it on its own.

1. **Monsoon** – jungle river delta, boss GOLIATH
2. **Foundry** – robot factory rooftops, boss HARBINGER
3. **The Hive** – alien caves, boss the HIVE QUEEN
4. **Dune Sea** – desert, boss the DUNE WYRM
5. **Sun Temple** – ancient temple, boss the SCORPION KING
6. **Whiteout** – snowy peaks, boss AVALANCHE
7. **Glacier Base** – ice fortress, boss the CRYO CANNON
8. **Skyline** – city streets and rooftops, boss WRECKER
9. **Metro** – subway tunnels and a ride on top of a speeding train, boss the IRON EXPRESS
10. **Magma Fields** – volcano, boss the INFERNO DRAKE
11. **The Forge** – conveyor belts and presses, boss the FORGE MASTER
12. **Stratos** – floating sky islands, boss STORMBIRD
13. **Iron Heart** – the flying fortress, the final battle

## Full screen
- On a computer, press the full-screen button at the top right of the title screen, or turn on **FULL SCREEN** in the pause menu.
- On a phone or tablet, the first tap switches to full screen. For the best experience, use **Add to Home Screen** (Safari: Share button). The game then opens like an app, full screen and sideways.

## Boss fights
Every boss fights in three stages. When its health bar passes a white mark, it goes into **FURY**: it roars, blows you back, gets faster and calls in flying robots. In the last stage (**FINAL FURY**) red marks appear on the floor, and rocks crash down on them a moment later, so keep moving.

## Online co-op
Play together on two computers, phones or tablets, anywhere.
1. Both players open https://ahamze123.github.io/iron-monsoon/ and pick **ONLINE CO-OP** on the title screen.
2. One player picks **HOST A GAME** and gets a 4-letter room code.
3. The other player picks **JOIN A GAME** and types that code (or taps the arrows on a phone).
4. The host picks the mission, mode and health, then presses **START!**

The host plays Volt (blue) and the friend plays Ember (red). Both screens show the same game. Pause opens a small menu while the game keeps running. Online play needs an internet connection and works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

## Controls
- **Keyboard (1 player):** arrows or WASD to move and aim, **J** or **Z** to fire, **K**, **X** or **Space** to jump (press again in the air to double jump), **Q** to switch guns, **E** for Super Soldier, **L** or **Shift** to lock your aim, **P** to pause.
- **Slide:** run, hold down and press jump. You slide under bullets and kick small enemies.
- **2 players on one keyboard:** P1 uses WASD + F fire + G jump + R switch gun + T super. P2 uses the arrows + J fire + K jump + U switch gun + I super.
- **Gamepad:** stick or d-pad moves, **A** jumps, **X** fires, **Y** switches guns, **B** is Super Soldier, **RB** locks your aim, **Start** pauses.
- **Touch:** left thumb moves and aims. On the right are FIRE, JUMP, the gun button (switch guns), SUPER, and AUTO (auto-fire).

## Super Soldier
Defeating robots, grabbing power-ups, freeing Bolt bots and hitting bosses fills your **SUPER** bar. When it is full, press **E** (or **B** on a gamepad, or tap **SUPER**). For 10 seconds you glow gold, nothing can hurt you, you run faster and jump higher, your gun fires at full power, and golden nova bolts fly through everything.

## Switching guns
Every gun you pick up stays with you. Press **Q** (or **Y**, or the gun button) to switch between them. The little hexagons in your panel show the guns you carry.

## Vehicles
Every mission has vehicles waiting for you. Walk into one to climb in, and press **down + jump** to hop off.
- **STORM BIKE** is very fast, jumps far and rams robots. Its twin blasters fire forward.
- **BOLT TANK** is tough. It has a big cannon you can aim and a machine gun, and it can drive right across lava and acid.
- **SKY JET** flies anywhere on the screen and fires cannons and homing missiles.

Vehicles have their own armour. When it runs out, the vehicle blows up and you jump clear.

## Power-ups
Every power-up shows its name and a short description when you grab it.
- **Guns:** Pulse, Scatter, Arc, Firestorm, Hornet, Rail, Grenade and Blade. Grab the same gun again to raise it to level 2 and level 3.
- **Shield** puts a bubble around you that blocks all hits, **Medkit** restores your health, **Speed** makes you run fast, **Rapid Fire** doubles your fire rate, **Mega Bomb** clears the screen, **Bolt Buddy** is a helper drone that shoots for you, **Star** makes you invincible, and **1UP** gives an extra life.

## Settings
- **Mode:** Recruit (3 hits per life, extra lives, keep your gun) or Veteran (classic one-hit rules).
- **Health:** Normal or Unlimited, so younger players can practise without losing.
- Missions give 1 to 3 stars: finish for one, lose few lives for two, lose none and free every caged Bolt bot for three.
