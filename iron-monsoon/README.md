# Iron Monsoon

An original run-and-gun adventure in bright painted worlds, for 1 to 4 players. Two heroes, Volt and Ember, fight the robot army of the Overseer. **Play:** https://ahamze123.github.io/iron-monsoon/

## Story and missions
Pick **STORY MODE** on the title screen to play the whole adventure, with Volt, Ember, Colonel Hawk, Dr. Nova and the little helper drone Bolt talking between missions. You can also pick any mission you have reached and play it on its own.

1. **Monsoon** – jungle river delta, boss GOLIATH
2. **Foundry** – robot factory rooftops, boss HARBINGER
3. **The Hive** – alien caves, boss the HIVE QUEEN
4. **Dune Sea** – desert, boss the DUNE WYRM
5. **Sun Temple** – ancient temple, boss the SCORPION KING
6. **Whiteout** – snowy peaks, boss AVALANCHE
7. **Glacier Base** – ice fortress, boss the CRYO CANNON
8. **Skyline** – city streets and rooftops, boss WRECKER
9. **Metro** – subway tunnels and a ride on top of a speeding train, boss the IRON EXPRESS
10. **Magma Fields** – volcano, boss the INFERNO DRAKE
11. **The Forge** – conveyor belts and presses, boss the FORGE MASTER
12. **Stratos** – floating sky islands, boss STORMBIRD
13. **Iron Heart** – the flying fortress, the final battle

## Your own hero
Every child can have their own hero (up to 8). Pick **MY HERO** on the title screen:
- Type a **name**, and choose the **armour** colour, the **scarf** colour and a **helmet** extra (antenna, fin, horns, cat ears, star or crown).
- **Outfits** unlock with the stars you earn in missions: Night Ops, Arctic, Lava, Chrome, Ghost, Rainbow and the Golden Hero.
- Each hero keeps their own coins, stars, story chapter, shop upgrades and trophies. With 2, 3 or 4 players, each player picks their own hero (◂ ▸ on **EDITING** switches between the players).

## Coins and the shop
Robots drop **coins**, freed Bolt bots give a few more, and every boss bursts into a shower of coins that flies straight to you. Spend them in the **SHOP**:
- **Tough Armour** (more hearts), **Extra Life**, **Super Charger** (the SUPER bar fills faster), **Coin Magnet**, **Vehicle Armour** and a **Start Shield**.
- **Guns** you buy (Scatter, Grenade, Hornet, Arc, Blade, Rail, Firestorm) are in your hands at the start of every mission.

## Trophies
The **TROPHIES** wall has 22 trophies to win, like beating a boss without getting hit, freeing 25 Bolt bots, a 25 combo, riding every vehicle and collecting 1,000 coins. A thin bar under each trophy shows how close you are.

## Full screen
- On a computer, press the full-screen button at the top right of the title screen, or turn on **FULL SCREEN** in the pause menu.
- On a phone or tablet, the first tap switches to full screen. For the best experience, use **Add to Home Screen** (Safari: Share button). The game then opens like an app, full screen and sideways.

## More modes
Pick **MORE MODES** on the title screen:
- **BOSS RUSH:** all 13 bosses in a row against the clock. Your health comes back between bosses, and every third boss gives an extra life. Each hero keeps their best time.
- **SURVIVAL:** endless waves of robots in the world you pick (◂ ▸). Every fifth wave brings that world's mini-boss. Each hero keeps their best wave for every world.
- **SCORE BATTLE:** 2 to 4 players, three minutes. The highest score wins (the leader wears a crown).
- **COIN RACE:** 2 to 4 players, two minutes. Robots drop double coins, and the most coins wins.

Online, the host can pick any of these under **GAME** in the lobby.

## Mini-bosses
Halfway through every mission a mini-boss blocks the way: the **CRAB TANK**, **HOVER GUNSHIP**, **SHIELD TITAN**, **SPIDER MECH**, **FROST WALKER** and **MAGMA GOLEM**. Each one has a glowing weak spot that takes extra damage, and each gets angry below half health. The Shield Titan blocks shots with its shield, so hit its back when it turns round to fire.

## Secrets
- Every mission hides the seven golden letters **M-O-N-S-O-O-N**. They show at the top of the screen; letters a hero found before are see-through. Find all seven in one game for an extra life and a shower of coins.
- Two **treasure chests** wait on high ledges in every mission. Shoot them open for a pile of coins.

## Boss fights
Every boss fights in three stages. When its health bar passes a white mark, it goes into **FURY**: it roars, blows you back, gets faster and calls in flying robots. In the last stage (**FINAL FURY**) red marks appear on the floor, and rocks crash down on them a moment later, so keep moving.

## Online co-op (up to 4 players)
Play together on up to four computers, phones or tablets, anywhere.
1. Everyone opens https://ahamze123.github.io/iron-monsoon/ and picks **ONLINE CO-OP** on the title screen.
2. One player picks **HOST A GAME** and gets a 4-letter room code.
3. Up to three friends pick **JOIN A GAME** and type that code (or tap the arrows on a phone). The lobby shows who is in the room.
4. The host picks the game, mission, mode and health, then presses **START!** (as soon as one friend is there).

Your own hero answers your buttons at once (within a sixtieth of a second or two). For a moment the game guesses what your friends are pressing, and when their real buttons arrive it quietly corrects itself, so every screen shows the same game with almost no delay. Each player brings their own hero, with their name, colours and shop upgrades; coins and trophies go to the hero on your own screen, and your own panel has a bright edge. Every screen shows the same game. Two heroes that look the same, or have the same name, get told apart automatically. Pause opens a small menu while the game keeps running. If a friend leaves in the middle, their hero leaves too and the game goes on. Online play needs an internet connection and works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

## Controls
- **Keyboard (1 player):** arrows or WASD to move and aim, **J** or **Z** to fire, **K**, **X** or **Space** to jump (press again in the air to double jump), **Q** to switch guns, **E** for Super Soldier, **L** or **Shift** to lock your aim, **P** to pause.
- **Slide:** run, hold down and press jump. You slide under bullets and kick small enemies.
- **2 players on one keyboard:** P1 uses WASD + F fire + G jump + R switch gun + T super. P2 uses the arrows + J fire + K jump + U switch gun + I super.
- **3 or 4 players on one computer:** set **PLAYERS** to 3 or 4. P1 and P2 share the keyboard as above, P3 plays with the first gamepad and P4 with the second (more gamepads go to P1 and P2). Press a button on each gamepad so the game can see it. The panels of all heroes sit in a row at the top; with more heroes, ambushes bring more robots and bosses get tougher.
- **Gamepad:** stick or d-pad moves, **A** jumps, **X** fires, **Y** switches guns, **B** is Super Soldier, **RB** locks your aim, **Start** pauses.
- **Touch:** left thumb moves and aims. On the right are FIRE, JUMP, the gun button (switch guns), SUPER, and AUTO (auto-fire).

## Super Soldier
Defeating robots, grabbing power-ups, freeing Bolt bots and hitting bosses fills your **SUPER** bar. When it is full, press **E** (or **B** on a gamepad, or tap **SUPER**). For 10 seconds you glow gold, nothing can hurt you, you run faster and jump higher, your gun fires at full power, and golden nova bolts fly through everything.

## Switching guns
Every gun you pick up stays with you. Press **Q** (or **Y**, or the gun button) to switch between them. The little hexagons in your panel show the guns you carry.

## Vehicles
Every mission has vehicles waiting for you: six kinds in all. Walk into one to climb in, and press **down + jump** to hop off.
- **STORM BIKE** is very fast, jumps far and rams robots. Its twin blasters fire forward.
- **BOLT TANK** is tough. It has a big cannon you can aim and a machine gun, and it can drive right across lava and acid.
- **SKY JET** flies anywhere on the screen and fires cannons and homing missiles.
- **MECH WALKER** stomps robots flat, walks over lava, steps right out of rivers, hops with its jets and has a heavy gatling arm.
- **JET-SKI** zooms across the water (slower on land) with twin blasters.
- **SNOWMOBILE** races over the snow and skims across icy water.

Vehicles have their own armour. When it runs out, the vehicle blows up and you jump clear.

## Power-ups
Every power-up shows its name and a short description when you grab it.
- **Guns:** Pulse, Scatter, Arc, Firestorm, Hornet, Rail, Grenade and Blade. Grab the same gun again to raise it to level 2 and level 3.
- **Shield** puts a bubble around you that blocks all hits, **Medkit** restores your health, **Speed** makes you run fast, **Rapid Fire** doubles your fire rate, **Mega Bomb** clears the screen, **Bolt Buddy** is a helper drone that shoots for you, **Star** makes you invincible, and **1UP** gives an extra life.

## Settings
- **Mode:** Recruit (3 hits per life, extra lives, keep your gun) or Veteran (classic one-hit rules).
- **Health:** Normal or Unlimited, so younger players can practise without losing.
- Missions give 1 to 3 stars: finish for one, lose few lives for two, lose none and free every caged Bolt bot for three.
- **OPTIONS** on the title screen holds the mode, health, sound and music settings.
