# Iron Monsoon

An original run-and-gun adventure in bright painted worlds. Two heroes, Volt and Ember, fight the robot army of the Overseer. **Play:** https://ahamze123.github.io/iron-monsoon/

## Story and missions
Pick **STORY MODE** on the title screen to play the whole adventure, with Volt, Ember, Colonel Hawk, Dr. Nova and the little helper drone Bolt talking between missions. You can also pick any mission you have reached and play it on its own.

1. **Monsoon** – jungle river delta, boss GOLIATH
2. **Foundry** – robot factory rooftops, boss HARBINGER
3. **The Hive** – alien caves, boss the HIVE QUEEN
4. **Dune Sea** – desert, boss the DUNE WYRM
5. **Sun Temple** – ancient temple, boss the SCORPION KING
6. **Whiteout** – snowy peaks, boss AVALANCHE
7. **Glacier Base** – ice fortress, boss the CRYO CANNON
8. **Skyline** – city streets and rooftops, boss WRECKER
9. **Metro** – subway tunnels and a ride on top of a speeding train, boss the IRON EXPRESS
10. **Magma Fields** – volcano, boss the INFERNO DRAKE
11. **The Forge** – conveyor belts and presses, boss the FORGE MASTER
12. **Stratos** – floating sky islands, boss STORMBIRD
13. **Iron Heart** – the flying fortress, the final battle

## Full screen
- On a computer, press the full-screen button at the top right of the title screen, or turn on **FULL SCREEN** in the pause menu.
- On a phone or tablet, the first tap switches to full screen. For the best experience, use **Add to Home Screen** (Safari: Share button). The game then opens like an app, full screen and sideways.

## Controls
- **Keyboard (1 player):** arrows or WASD to move and aim, **J** or **Z** to fire, **K**, **X** or **Space** to jump (press again in the air to double jump), **L** or **Shift** to lock your aim, **P** to pause.
- **Slide:** run, hold down and press jump. You slide under bullets and kick small enemies.
- **2 players on one keyboard:** P1 uses WASD + F fire + G jump, P2 uses the arrows + J fire + K jump. Gamepads work too.
- **Touch:** left thumb moves and aims, FIRE and JUMP buttons on the right, AUTO turns on auto-fire.

## Power-ups
Every power-up shows its name and a short description when you grab it.
- **Guns:** Pulse, Scatter, Arc, Firestorm, Hornet, Rail, Grenade and Blade. Grab the same gun again to raise it to level 2 and level 3.
- **Shield** blocks all hits, **Medkit** restores your health, **Speed** makes you run fast, **Rapid Fire** doubles your fire rate, **Mega Bomb** clears the screen, **Bolt Buddy** is a helper drone that shoots for you, **Star** makes you invincible, and **1UP** gives an extra life.

## Settings
- **Mode:** Recruit (3 hits per life, extra lives, keep your gun) or Veteran (classic one-hit rules).
- **Health:** Normal or Unlimited, so younger players can practise without losing.
- Missions give 1 to 3 stars: finish for one, lose few lives for two, lose none and free every caged Bolt bot for three.
