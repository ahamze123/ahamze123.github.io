# Flop Island

Wobbly fuzzy animals on a big sunny island, and floppy brawls. **Play:** https://ahamze123.github.io/flop-island/

Your animal stands on a little stage on the title screen, so you always see your look before you play. **Dress Up** changes it right there.

## The story: the Great Flop Festival
The islanders are getting ready for their yearly festival, but Boss Grumble (a grumpy raccoon) and his Grumble Gang keep causing trouble. Eleven chapters take you all over the island, ending with a boss brawl at the stadium and a big party in the plaza. The **Story Book** (title screen and pause menu) shows every chapter, and **Show me the way** points to the next islander.

## Jobs, levels and unlocks
- **15 jobs**, each with a Job Board: parcels, pizza, juice stand, carrots, sheep, block builder, garbage truck, firefighter, lifeguard, police patrol, ice cream van, taxi, crystal miner, time trial and Wobble Run. Each job has ranks, from Rookie to Pro, Star and Legend, and higher ranks pay more.
- **Levels 1–50:** XP from jobs, missions, the story, brawls, ducks and gifts. There's a prize every 5 levels.
- **Unlocks:** you start with Pup, Kitty and Bunny, and the other animals, jobs, bus stops and special vehicles open as you play. You can buy vehicles at the Car Shop.
- **20 gift boxes** hide around the island, plus 16 golden ducks.

## Looks
- **17 animals:** pup, kitty, bunny, bear, duck, frog, piggy, fox, panda, dino, shark, penguin, cow, raccoon, monkey, unicorn and lion.
- **Clothes:** about 55 clothes for the face, neck, top and back, lots of hats, and 18 full costumes (astronaut, pirate, knight, wizard, ninja, superhero, princess…).
- **Shops:** the Fluff & Stuff clothes shop and the Hat Shop. Dress Up has tabs for every slot.

## Around the island
- **Pine Hills:** buy a house (cabin, cottage, villa or a two-floor mansion), furnish it from the furniture shop, and get a pet from the pet shop (puppy, kitten, bunny, parrot, piglet, duckling). Pets follow you and fetch.
- **Museum Isle:** a treasure hunt with map clues (dig up 12 treasures for the museum), fishing (15 kinds of fish, with a Fish Book), and an arcade with Hide & Seek, Trash Zone and Sandbox.
- **Space Center:** ride the rocket to the Moon (low gravity!) and collect 10 moon rocks.
- **Day and night:** a full day lasts 12 minutes, with lamp posts and fireflies at night, rain showers, rainbows, and snow near the top of Mount Wobble.
- **Vehicles:** cars, a tractor, a race car, a buggy and a truck, plus boats, jet skis, a helicopter, a plane on the airstrip, bikes, scooters, a bus, a monster truck, and the job vehicles. Bus stops (on the map) take you across the island.

## Brawl Party
- **13 arenas:** Rooftop Rumble, Tipsy Iceberg, Spin Cake, Truck Tops, Ferris Wheel, Subway Rush, Crane Girders, Lift Shafts, Storm Boat, Wind Tunnel, Breaking Ice, Croc Pond and Falling Bridge.
- **6 modes:** Last Stand, Gang Brawl (teams), Waves (all together against CPU baddies), Football, Hockey and Gold Rush. **Items** drops cartoon weapons into the arena: foam bat, frying pan, squeaky hammer, rubber chicken, water gun, water balloon, fizz bomb, boxing glove and fish.
- **Moves:** punch, grab, throw and dropkick, plus headbutt (hold PUNCH), slide tackle, body slam, backflip, lift and throw far, duck and crawl, go floppy, 8 emotes and a victory pose. Mash buttons to wake up faster.

## Play online with friends
Each friend plays on their own tablet. One taps **Play Online with Friends → Host a game**, and the others tap that game in the list (or type its 4-letter code). Up to **8 friends** play together, with CPU animals too. You can explore the island together, or the host starts any brawl mode. If you're knocked out, the camera watches a friend until the next round.

The tablets find each other through free public message servers (MQTT and ntfy.sh) and then connect directly. If they can't connect directly, the game goes through those servers instead.

## Controls
| | Touch | Keyboard | Game controller |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | left side stick | WASD or arrows | left stick |
| Jump / climb | JUMP | Space | A |
| Punch / throw (hold: headbutt or lift) | PUNCH | F | X |
| Grab (hold) | GRAB | G | RB / RT |
| Dropkick (sprinting: slide; in the air: body slam) | KICK | H | B |
| Duck / crawl (twice and hold: go floppy) | DUCK | C (Z: floppy) | LT |
| Emotes | EMOTE | T, then 1–8 | d-pad |
| Use, drive, talk | USE | E | Y |
| Look around | drag the right side | mouse, Q / R | right stick |
| Map | tap the round map | M | View |

**Pro hands** (on the How to Play screen) lets each paw punch and grab on its own.

## On an iPad
1. Open the link in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open Flop Island from the Home Screen icon. It fills the whole screen. There's also a **Full screen** button on the title screen.

The game is saved on the tablet. The Home Screen app keeps its own save, separate from Safari.
