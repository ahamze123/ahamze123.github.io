# Flop Island

Wobbly fuzzy animals on a sunny island, and big floppy brawls. **Play:** https://ahamze123.github.io/flop-island/

## Explore the Island
- Walk, climb, swim, jump on trampolines, fire yourself out of the cannon and drive the cars.
- **Jobs pay coins:** carry parcels from the Delivery Depot to the glowing house before time runs out, or punch palm trees and drop the coconuts in the juice basket.
- **Missions:** islanders with a yellow **!** over their heads need a hand: catch a thief, rush a pizza, round up sheep, build a tower, race a lap, climb Mount Wobble, find the treasure in the Crystal Cave, rescue swimmers and more. Many give a prize hat.
- 16 golden ducks hide around the island. Coins buy hats at the Hat Shop.
- The round map in the corner shows where you are (tap it for the big map). Yellow bus stops take you across the island.

## Brawl Party
Last animal standing wins the round: punch, grab, dropkick and throw the others off the arena (Rooftop Rumble, Tipsy Iceberg or Spin Cake), against CPU animals or friends.

## Play Online with Friends
Each friend plays on their own tablet. One taps **Play Online with Friends → Host a game**, the others tap that game in the list (or type its 4-letter code). Up to 4 friends play together, with CPU animals too: explore the island together, or the host starts a brawl for everyone.

The tablets find each other through free public message servers (MQTT and ntfy.sh) and then connect directly. If they can't connect directly (some Wi-Fi keeps devices apart), the game goes through those servers instead.

## Controls
| | Touch | Keyboard | Game controller |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | left side stick | WASD or arrows | left stick |
| Jump / climb | JUMP | Space | A |
| Punch (throw while holding) | PUNCH | F | X |
| Grab (hold) | GRAB | G | RB / RT |
| Dropkick | KICK | H | B |
| Use, drive, talk | USE | E | Y |
| Look around | drag the right side | mouse, Q / R | right stick |
| Map | tap the round map | M | View |

## On an iPad
1. Open the link in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open Flop Island from the Home Screen icon: it fills the whole screen.

The game is saved on the tablet. The Home Screen app keeps its own save, separate from Safari.
