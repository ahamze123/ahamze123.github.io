# Street Monsoon

A street brawler for 1 to 4 players, made from Iron Monsoon's engine. Volt, Ember and Col. Hawk fight the Chrome Jackals gang across ten stages in two parts to save Skyline City. **Play:** https://ahamze123.github.io/street-monsoon/

## The fighters
- **VOLT** (balanced): fast punches, a rising flying kick, and the special **THUNDER BALL**: he gathers lightning in his hands and throws it across the street.
- **EMBER** (speed): the quickest kicks, a spin kick that clears a crowd, and the special **BLAZE TORNADO**: she spins through the air and fire bursts up under every thug.
- **COL. HAWK** (power): slow but mighty, with a push kick that sends thugs flying into each other, and the special **EARTH SHAKER**: he slams the ground and a shock wave throws every thug into the air.

## The streets
- **Part 1:** Main Street, the Docks, Neon Nights and King's Tower, where the King waits on the roof.
- **Part 2:** the Underground, Skyline Mall, the Fun Park, Frosty Park, the Building Site and the Grand Theater. Each has its own boss who also throws things across the street: Sparks (spark balls), Roller Queen (spinning stars), Big Top Bruno (dumbbells), Icebreaker (snowballs), Big Buster (bricks) and The Maestro (music notes).
- Every stage has a mini-boss halfway, and every boss gets a **FURY** when hurt (faster, stronger, and things fall from the sky).

## How to play
- **Punch** and **Kick** each have their own combo, and you can punch and kick while walking. Run (tap a direction twice) and punch or kick for a dash attack. Jump and punch or kick for a flying attack.
- Walk into a thug to **grab** him: punch for knee strikes, kick (or punch after two knees) to throw him into his friends.
- Every blow that lands fills your **SUPER** bar. When it is full, press **Special** for your hero's special attack, which hits every thug on the screen.
- Crates, barrels and treasure chests hide food (health), coins, cash, gems, a star (fills the SUPER bar) and extra lives. Seven golden letters, **M-O-N-S-O-O-N**, are hidden in every stage.
- Each stage gives up to 3 stars. Stars unlock outfits; coins buy upgrades in the **SHOP** (more health, harder hits, a faster SUPER bar and more). **MY HERO** keeps each player's own name, fighter, outfit, coins and trophies.

## Modes
- **STORY MODE:** both parts in order, with the story between stages.
- **PLAY STAGE:** any stage you have reached.
- **MORE MODES:** **BOSS RUSH** (every boss in a row, against the clock), **SURVIVAL** (endless waves), and for 2 to 4 players **SCORE BATTLE** and **COIN RACE**.
- **HEALTH: UNLIMITED** (in OPTIONS and the pause menu) for younger players.

## Controls
- **Keyboard (1 player):** arrows or WASD to move, **J** punch, **K** kick, **L** or **Space** jump, **I** special. (With the arrows you can also use Z punch, X kick, C jump, V special.) **P** or **Esc** pauses.
- **2 players on one keyboard:** P1 uses WASD + **F** punch, **G** kick, **H** jump, **T** special. P2 uses the arrows + **J** punch, **K** kick, **L** jump, **I** special.
- **3 or 4 players on one computer:** players 3 and 4 use gamepads (players 1 and 2 keep the keyboard).
- **Gamepad:** stick or d-pad moves, **X** punch, **Y** kick, **A** jump, **B** (or a shoulder button) special, **Start** pauses.
- **Touch:** left thumb moves; on the right are PUNCH, KICK, JUMP and SPECIAL (the ring round SPECIAL is your SUPER bar).

## Online co-op (up to 4 players)
1. Everyone opens https://ahamze123.github.io/street-monsoon/ and picks **ONLINE CO-OP** on the title screen.
2. One player picks **HOST A GAME** and gets a 4-letter room code.
3. Friends pick **JOIN A GAME** and type that code (or tap ▲ ▼ on each letter). Two players may pick the same fighter: the second one wears other colours.
4. The host picks the level and presses **START!**.

The host's computer runs the game; friends' buttons go to it and every screen shows the same fight, with the same sounds and effects. Your own fighter moves the moment you press, even on a slow connection. If a friend leaves, their hero leaves and the game goes on. The menu (P or Esc) does not stop the game for the others. Online play needs an internet connection and works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

## Full screen
- The game switches to full screen when you start playing (turn it off with **FULL SCREEN** on the title screen or in the pause menu).
- On an iPad or iPhone, for the best full screen: open the page in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. The game then opens like an app, full screen and sideways.

## How it was made
The fighters and the six Part 2 bosses were drawn in ChatGPT from the front, side and back, turned into 3D models and animated with Meshy, then drawn as cel-shaded pictures for every move. Every other picture was drawn in ChatGPT too: the ten streets (skylines, buildings, floors), the lamps, benches, boats and rooftop gear, the neon signs, the crates, barrels and chests, and the food and treasure you pick up.
