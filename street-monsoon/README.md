# Street Monsoon

A street brawler for 1 to 3 players, made from Iron Monsoon's engine. Volt, Ember and Col. Hawk fight the Chrome Jackals gang across four stages to save Skyline City. **Play:** https://ahamze123.github.io/street-monsoon/

## The fighters
- **VOLT** (balanced): fast punches, a rising flying kick, and the special **THUNDER BALL**: he gathers lightning in his hands and throws it across the street.
- **EMBER** (speed): the quickest kicks, a spin kick that clears a crowd, and the special **BLAZE TORNADO**: she spins through the air and fire bursts up under every thug.
- **COL. HAWK** (power): slow but mighty, with a push kick that sends thugs flying into each other, and the special **EARTH SHAKER**: he slams the ground and a shock wave throws every thug into the air.

The fighters were drawn in ChatGPT, turned into 3D models and animated with Meshy, then drawn as cel-shaded pictures for every move.

## How to play
- **Punch** and **Kick** each have their own combo; keep hitting to chain the next blow. Run (tap a direction twice) and punch or kick for a dash attack. Jump and punch or kick for a flying attack.
- Walk into a thug to **grab** him: punch for knee strikes, kick (or punch after two knees) to throw him into his friends.
- Every blow that lands fills your **SUPER** bar. When it is full, press **Special** for your hero's special attack, which hits every thug on the screen.
- Crates and barrels hide food (health), cash, gems, a star (fills the SUPER bar) and extra lives.

## Controls
- **Keyboard (1 player):** arrows or WASD to move, **J** punch, **K** kick, **L** or **Space** jump, **I** special. (With the arrows you can also use Z punch, X kick, C jump, V special.) **P** or **Esc** pauses.
- **2 players on one keyboard:** P1 uses WASD + **F** punch, **G** kick, **H** jump, **T** special. P2 uses the arrows + **J** punch, **K** kick, **L** jump, **I** special.
- **Gamepad:** stick or d-pad moves, **X** punch, **Y** kick, **A** jump, **B** (or a shoulder button) special, **Start** pauses.
- **Touch:** left thumb moves; on the right are PUNCH, KICK, JUMP and SPECIAL (the ring round SPECIAL is your SUPER bar).

## Online co-op (up to 3 players)
1. Everyone opens https://ahamze123.github.io/street-monsoon/ and picks **ONLINE CO-OP** on the title screen.
2. One player picks **HOST A GAME** and gets a 4-letter room code.
3. Friends pick **JOIN A GAME** and type that code (or tap ▲ ▼ on each letter). Each player picks a different fighter.
4. The host picks the level and presses **START!**.

The host's computer runs the game; friends' buttons go to it and every screen shows the same fight, with the same sounds and effects. If a friend leaves, their hero leaves and the game goes on. The menu (P or Esc) does not stop the game for the others. Online play needs an internet connection and works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

## Full screen
- The game switches to full screen when you start playing (turn it off with **FULL SCREEN** on the title screen or in the pause menu).
- On an iPad or iPhone, for the best full screen: open the page in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. The game then opens like an app, full screen and sideways.
