ShiftSquad

Pick a workplace and work at your own pace. There is no clock and no customer timer. Robots line up with tickets and wait for you until the host clocks out. Up to six coworkers share one shift in the same workplace.

Worker: serve the robots in line. Customer: walk up to the counter and order, and your crew serves you.
Hosting gives you a room code to share. Friends enter it under Join and work in the same workplace.

----
Now and then a customer also asks for one item from another shop and pays a bonus for it. It is never required to finish the order.
    1. The whole room works in the same workplace. The host picks it above.
    2. Town: walk down the street and press the button at a shop door to go in. Each shop works like its own workplace. The green door at the back right takes you back out. The number over a door shows how many robots are waiting there.
    3. Each robot shows what it needs on a ticket. Grab it from your shelves, work it at the station in the middle of the floor (scan, cook, label or inspect), then hand it over.
    4. When the ticket is complete, press the button at that station to finish. The garage needs three turns.
    5. Bonus coins: at your station a bar slides back and forth. Press while it is in the green for a Perfect. Back-to-back Perfects pay more, and a miss costs nothing.
    6. A 👑 VIP robot orders more and pays double. A ⚡ speedy robot pays 50% extra if you finish quickly. Nobody ever leaves angry, so there is no penalty for being slow.
    7. Spend banked coins in the 🪙 Shop. Upgrades help only you. A robot dog fetches items for you (press R), and decorations are seen by everyone in the room.
    8. You can walk anywhere to help a coworker. Tap 👁 to watch the room from above, and 📷 to take a photo.
    9. Pick Customer on the start screen or in the break room, or tap 🛒 (or press C) during a shift, to be a customer: pick up to three things and your crew serves you while you look around. In Town you pick the shop. If nobody else is there, a helper robot serves you for half pay.
    10. No timers anywhere. Customers wait as long as it takes, and the host clocks out when the crew is done.
