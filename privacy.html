Privacy Policy

Block Buddies: Brick Heroes · last updated 3 October 2026

Block Buddies is a family-made game for children and grown-ups. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

Your game stays on your device

Your progress (heroes, coins, pets, missions) is saved only on your own iPad. We never receive it. "Move my game" makes a game code that stays on your device until you paste it somewhere yourself.

Playing online with family and friends (optional)

  • Online play only connects tablets that use the same family code, which you choose and share yourself.
  • To find each other, the tablets use free public connection services: the PeerJS server, public MQTT message servers (Eclipse, EMQX and HiveMQ), and connection helpers from Google, Cloudflare and Open Relay. These services pass short connection messages in real time and may see your device's internet address, under their own privacy policies. We do not run them, and we do not store anything that passes through them.
  • While playing together, the game sends your hero's name, hero and what happens in the game directly to the other players. Use a nickname as your hero's name, not a full name.
  • There is no chat. Players can only send emoji reactions.

Voices and sounds

People in the game speak with your device's built-in voices. This happens on your device.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Parents can choose whether online play is used, and the family code keeps online games among the tablets that know it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page. Questions: see the support page.
