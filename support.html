Block Buddies: Brick Heroes – Help

A brick hero adventure for kids, made by a family.

Playing together online

  1. On every tablet that plays together, open Play online and tap Change next to Family code.
  2. Type the same code on all of them (4 to 12 letters or numbers) and tap Save.
  3. One player taps Host a game; the others tap Join next to their name.

Only tablets with the same family code can see your games. Everyone needs the internet; the same Wi-Fi works best.

The full game (App Store app)

The app is free to start: Brick City, Mushroom Forest and the first two story chapters. A grown-up can open everything else with one purchase: Menu → ⭐ The full game → For grown-ups. Already bought it? On another iPad, open the same page and tap Restore purchase. With Family Sharing, the other iPads in your family get it too.

Bringing your game from the website

On the website, tap Menu → Settings → 💾 My game → Copy my game. Then in the app tap Menu → Settings → 💾 My game, paste the code and tap Load this game.

Keeping your game safe

Your game is saved on your iPad only. Menu → Settings → 💾 My game → Save a copy keeps a copy in Files.

Sound

Menu → Settings turns the sound, the music, the voices and the footsteps on or off.
